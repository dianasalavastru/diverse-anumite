# Homepage — High-Fidelity Specification (measured reality)

**A validation exercise, designed from a blank page against `Documentation v1.0` and `VISUAL_DIRECTION_v2.0`.** Not a production implementation. Its purpose is to prove the philosophy is understood by letting every major decision *emerge* from it.

Status: design-phase validation · 2026-07-30 · supersedes nothing (a fresh design)

**The one-line concept.** The homepage is **a single continuous survey of the practice** — the visitor moves down it the way a scan moves through a site: arriving, orienting, measuring, bringing work into focus, inspecting, and arriving at a conversation. It is **one flowing composition**, not a stack of sections. The frozen modules (M-1…M-7) are still all present and unchanged in responsibility — but the visitor never feels a seam between them.

**Brand anchors, always on:** white ground; the pixel voice as identity and coordinate marks; electric blue *only* as the signal of activation and measurement; the mascot (giraffe / drone) as a warm character; floating compositions; the technical/digital aesthetic in service of the architecture, never as the hero. Warm, never cold.

**Usability stance (non-negotiable — this is a commercial studio site).** The task-first Global Header is always present and obvious; the paths to the pillars, the work, and contact are never hidden behind obscure gestures; the single primary action is unmistakable; focus interactions are keyboard-operable and never hijack the scroll; reduced-motion is fully supported; nothing artful is allowed to block a visitor from understanding the practice or reaching contact. Artistry serves usability here; it never overrides it.

---

## Part A — The continuity system (what makes it *one* composition)

Four devices dissolve the seams between the frozen modules so the page reads as a single measured traverse rather than a sequence of blocks. These operate everywhere and are the reason this design "flows."

**1. The blue measurement thread.** Blue obeys the §2.1 rule — it appears *only* at points of activation and measurement — but those points recur down the whole page (a dimension tick on the arrival image, the marker on the focused project, a value revealed on inspection, the one primary action). Read together, they form a single vertical thread the eye follows, like a scan progressing through the page. Blue is the connective tissue *because* it is rare and meaningful. *Why:* it gives continuity without decoration and turns the brand colour into the literal signal of the studio's craft.

**2. The coordinate index.** Movements are marked in the pixel voice as survey points — quiet coordinate/station labels (e.g. a small `01 / 02 / 03…` set in pixel numerals with a short label), threaded at one edge. They index the page as one continuous survey, not separate sections, and double as a calm scroll-position readout. *Why:* it expresses "measured reality" structurally and is a wayfinding aid (usability), while visually binding the movements.

**3. Overlap and bleed.** No movement ends on a hard horizontal edge. Imagery and elements cross the boundaries — an image from one movement bleeds into the white of the next; the next movement's first element begins before the previous has fully resolved. There are **no filled dividing bands** (the current navy/black bars are gone). *Why:* overlap is what makes a scroll read as continuous composition rather than stacked containers.

**4. One grid, one rhythm, continuous motion.** A single invisible asymmetric grid governs the entire page; a continuous (but varying) vertical rhythm runs through it; and as the visitor scrolls, elements ease into and out of focus smoothly, so the "instrument" feels like one coherent mechanism from top to bottom. *Why:* consistency of system is what lets compositional variety feel authored rather than random.

## Part B — The visual system, applied (concrete, brand-anchored)

*Behaviour and hierarchy, not code. Concrete enough to build against.*

**Ground & colour.** White is the working ground throughout — protected, no filled section bands. Ink is near-black and warm (never a cold pure black — warmth caveat). Electric blue is reserved entirely for activation/measurement per §2.1. A darker "capture" field is *not* used here (it remains a prototype candidate, unvalidated); the reality-capture work earns its presence through scale and focus on white instead.

**Type roles.** The **pixel voice** carries the wordmark, the coordinate index, section station-marks, numerals, and short metadata labels — used with real breathing room, never as body. A **calm, legible reading voice** (a neutral, slightly technical sans) carries the few lines of prose. **Metadata is measurement data** — exact, aligned, quietly technical, like an instrument readout (project · year · type · a spec). Hierarchy comes from scale, space, and focus — not weight escalation, not blue.

**Focus is the hierarchy engine.** Wherever more than one thing exists, exactly one is *in focus* (measured): larger, full contrast, carrying a blue measurement mark; the rest recede (smaller, lower contrast) but stay visible for context. Focus is often a **state** — it shifts as the visitor explores. This single grammar governs the capabilities, the work, and the curated collections alike.

**Motion (a deliberate material).** Instrument-grade: slow, weighted, eased; used where it aids understanding or discovery — a focused item easing up ≈1.0→1.05, a value surfacing on inspection, a point cloud turning slowly. Never loops, never performs, none load-bearing; reduced-motion fully honored (focus and reveals resolve instantly, statically, with no loss of meaning).

**Discovery is built in.** Inspection is a first-class behaviour: reveals surface information *as if measured* (a sensor reading appearing under attention), the reality-capture centrepiece is genuinely explorable, and focus invites the visitor to move it. The visitor is an inspector, not an observer.

## Part C — The continuous narrative (movement by movement)

*Each movement realizes a frozen module (noted), but is written as a moment in one flowing composition. For each: the experience · composition & focus · blue activation · motion, interaction & discovery · pacing · how it flows onward · responsive · why (from the philosophy).*

### Persistent — Global Header (frozen; never shape-shifts)

A thin, quiet presence: the pixel wordmark anchored to one edge, the task-first nav (Despre · Servicii · Proiecte · Contact · EN) small and edge-pinned, the mascot present at minimal scale. The coordinate index lives with it at the opposite edge. Over the arrival image the header is transparent with ink chosen for contrast; it settles to white as the survey begins — one quiet state change, never a reshape. *Why:* usability demands a persistent, obvious task-first path; the quiet treatment keeps it subordinate to the work; the coordinate index makes wayfinding part of the instrument.

### Movement I — Arrival *(realizes M-1 Identity: one practice)*

**Experience.** Not a hero. The visitor *enters* — mostly white silence, a great calm. The pixel wordmark *diverse anumite* sits as a spatial coordinate anchored off-centre to an edge; the mascot is present, warm and small; and **one dominant architectural photograph floats off-centre, continuing past the bottom of the first screen** so the page is already inviting a scroll. A short, confident identity line sits nearby as measured-data caption — a few words, not a paragraph.

**Composition & focus.** Asymmetric, edge-weighted; the single image is the focus; the wordmark and mascot are subordinate marks. Enormous negative space — the silence of an instrument at rest.

**Blue activation.** Exactly one blue moment: a small **measurement mark on the image** — a dimension tick or a scan-line fragment — signalling *this is measured reality*. Blue on nothing else here.

**Motion, interaction & discovery.** Stillness, then a barely-perceptible settle. The image extending below the fold is the discovery cue; the coordinate index shows there is more survey ahead. No scroll-jacking.

**Pacing.** The most silent, most spacious moment on the page — the intake of breath.

**Flows onward.** The dominant image **bleeds downward** into Movement II; the blue thread and coordinate index carry down. No divider.

**Responsive.** Wordmark, mascot, and image recompose into one edge-weighted opening; the image still breaks the fold; identity stays first and warm.

**Why.** Warmth + curiosity + *one practice*, established by composition, not a sentence (calm + discovery; measured reality; the image, not the interface, is the hero).

### Movement II — Two capabilities *(realizes M-2 Pillar branch: co-equal)*

**Experience.** Out of the arrival image, two capabilities emerge as **two coordinate nodes in counterpoint** — Architecture & Design and Reality Capture — each a pixel-type name anchored to opposite edges and opposite vertical positions, each with a floating image. They are equal but not identical: mirrored, balanced, in tension.

**Composition & focus.** Equality through *balance*, not duplication — equal type rank, equal image presence, opposite anchoring. Focus is interactive: attending to one capability **brings it into focus** (full contrast, its image eases up, a blue measurement mark appears); the other recedes but stays present. Neither is primary at rest.

**Blue activation.** Blue marks the *focused* capability only — the act of "measuring" that choice. Never on both at once, never as fill.

**Motion, interaction & discovery.** Slow focus transition on hover/keyboard; the mascot may shift to its contextual variant (giraffe / drone) as a capability takes focus — warmth doing a wayfinding job. Each name is the doorway to its hub (module CTA → hub, fixed). Discovery: the visitor *measures* each capability by focusing it.

**Pacing.** Balanced and open — a two-note chord after the single note of arrival.

**Flows onward.** The unfocused capability's image trails into the white that becomes Movement III; the coordinate index ticks to the next station.

**Responsive.** The counterpoint stacks without implying primacy — equal size, equal treatment, arbitrary order; focus becomes tap-to-measure.

**Why.** Co-equality (D3) expressed as balanced tension rather than twin tiles; focus as hierarchy; blue as the activation of a choice; the mascot carries warmth and wayfinding. No "choose your direction" copy — the composition *is* the invitation.

### Movement III — The practice, measured *(realizes M-3 Practice credibility: pillar-neutral)*

**Experience.** A denser, quieter passage — credibility presented as **measured facts**, not a KPI band. A short confident human line ("we measure and compose space" register — warm, not clinical), beside a small set of precise readouts in the pixel/measurement voice: years of practice · EU-cofinanced equipment · scan accuracy. Arranged asymmetrically in white, edge-aligned, like data noted in the field.

**Composition & focus.** The page's **dense beat**, set against silence on the other side of the grid. The facts are a quiet constellation; none shouts. Pillar-neutral — no pillar imagery enters.

**Blue activation.** At most a single blue tick on one measured value; restraint is the point.

**Motion, interaction & discovery.** Values may surface *as if read* on entry (a measured reveal), then rest. No animation for its own sake.

**Pacing.** Density — the deliberate compression between two open moments.

**Flows onward.** The measurement voice established here **carries directly into the work's metadata** below, tying credibility to the evidence.

**Responsive.** Line then readouts stack; the data stays exact and calm; trust reads before the work at any width.

**Why.** Evidence over claims, as *measured data* not a dashboard; warmth via the human line so precision never turns cold; the dense beat creates rhythm (calm + discovery; measured reality).

### Movement IV — The work, in focus *(realizes M-4 Pillar sections ×2: preview & proof)*

**Experience — the heart of the survey.** The work is explored, not displayed. Each pillar becomes a **focus traverse**: projects move through a single point of focus, one at a time. The **focused project dominates** — large, full contrast, a blue measurement mark, its metadata as an instrument readout; **neighbours recede** (smaller, lower contrast) but remain visible so the visitor feels the body of work around the one in focus. The two pillars are given **distinct compositional character** while equal in overall weight.

- **Architecture & Design** traverses buildings and interiors — colour work in focus, black-and-white context receding.
- **Reality Capture** traverses scans and drone documentation and includes **the explorable point cloud as its signature centrepiece** — the one project the visitor can genuinely inspect (turn slowly, look closer). This is the site's most memorable moment, and it is the *real work*, not an effect.

**Composition & focus.** The §2.2 mechanism at full expression. A large focus-to-support ratio; authored crops; wide-then-detail sequencing within a project. The two traverses are separated by silence, not a divider.

**Blue activation.** The measurement mark on the focused project; a value surfacing on inspection. On the point cloud, blue reads as the literal scan-line — the brand colour being exactly what it depicts.

**Motion, interaction & discovery.** Slow focus transitions (≈1.0→1.05 on the focused item); keyboard/drag/scroll-friendly traversal that never hijacks the page; the point cloud turns slowly under inspection; reveals surface measured metadata. This is where discovery is strongest. The module continuation to each Pillar Hub is a **quiet measured link**, not a button (fixed module CTA → hub; individual-item behaviour deferred, unchanged from v1.0).

**Pacing.** Rich but calm — density handled by showing *one thing at a time in focus*, with silence between the two traverses. The busiest content, made calm by focus.

**Flows onward.** The last focused project eases down into the curated collections; the coordinate index advances.

**Responsive.** Traverses become touch-swipeable focus strips; focus, hierarchy, and the point-cloud inspection all survive; the two pillars stay comparably weighted.

**Why.** Focus as hierarchy; discovery as inspection; motion as a material; measured metadata; the real work as hero (warmth caveat: technology reveals the architecture). Not a card grid, not a product slider — an exhibition traverse in the brand's own digital voice.

### Movement V — Curated bodies of work *(realizes M-5: Competitions; Professional Experience)*

**Experience.** Two curated entry points of deliberately different character, offset on a diagonal within the flow (not two equal cards). **Competitions** is **index-led** — a coordinate list of entries and years in the pixel voice that is itself the teaser (ideas as measured data). **Professional Experience** is **image-led** — one strong project in focus with a quiet "grouped by employer" measured note.

**Composition & focus.** Different registers, balanced overall weight; the index earns weight through scale and position, the image through focus. Diagonal placement keeps them part of the continuous composition.

**Blue activation.** On focus/inspection within either — a measured highlight.

**Motion, interaction & discovery.** The index invites exploration (hovering an entry brings its year/detail into focus, measured); the image entry focuses on attention. Module CTA → the respective Curated View (never straight to entries — v1.0 rule preserved).

**Pacing.** Medium — a lighter beat after the heart, before the final silence.

**Flows onward.** Resolves into the great silence that precedes the invitation.

**Responsive.** The two stack, each keeping its distinct character; the index stays scannable (usability).

**Why.** Curation with distinct character (v2.0); index-as-measured-data is deeply brand-native; focus and blue on activation only.

### Movement VI — The invitation *(realizes M-6 Contact: the single primary action)*

**Experience.** The exhale. The greatest silence on the page, then **one quiet, confident line** and the page's **single primary action, expressed as one blue activation** — the one true "activate" of the whole survey. Not a filled button block; a precise, unmistakable blue action in a field of white. Warm and human in wording.

**Composition & focus.** Maximum isolation; the single action is the sole focus. This is the only place blue is allowed to be fully confident *as the action*.

**Blue activation.** Here blue reaches its purpose — the primary action. Everything upstream taught the visitor to read blue as "activate"; now it means *begin the conversation*.

**Motion, interaction & discovery.** A calm settle; on the action, a precise measured response (no pulse, no urgency). Leads to Contact (which carries the service-aware form — no inline form here).

**Pacing.** Extreme silence — the resolution.

**Flows onward.** Into the quiet close.

**Responsive.** Line and single action centre-of-attention (not centre-of-layout necessarily); remains the last and only primary conversion action.

**Why.** The acquisition *path* without hard-selling (understanding before persuasion); the single action honoured by isolation and by the whole page's blue grammar converging on it; usability — it is impossible to miss.

### Movement VII — The close *(realizes M-7 Footer: orientation, compliance)*

**Experience.** A composed ending, not a utility strip. The **mascot returns large and warm** (closing the loop with the arrival), the wordmark, quiet **text links** for social and contact (never blue buttons), the EU-funding acknowledgment as a small measured note, the language toggle. The coordinate index resolves to its final station.

**Composition & focus.** Asymmetric, edge-anchored; utility register; nothing loud. Warmth via the mascot as the last thing seen.

**Blue activation.** None, or a single link activation — the thread quietly ends.

**Motion.** Still.

**Pacing.** A quiet resolution.

**Responsive.** Groups recompose; content unchanged; mascot still present.

**Why.** Persistent orientation + compliance + the EU-funding note living here (Step 7); the composed close preserves character and removes the SaaS-footer feeling; the mascot leaves the visitor with warmth.

## Part D — The discovery centrepiece (highlight)

The **explorable point cloud** inside Movement IV is the single most important original moment. It is the studio's actual measured work, presented calmly and large, that the visitor can *inspect* — turn slowly, look closer — with the blue scan-line as its natural signal. It is the clearest possible proof of the "measured reality" proposition (discovery embodied, not described), and it is the moment the visitor is most likely to remember. It must be calm and performant (a prototype concern), never a spinning gadget demo, and it must reveal a *real building* — the technology serving the architecture, per the warmth caveat.

---

## Part E — Responsive as re-composition (not stacking)

The page **re-composes** at each width; it never collapses into a monotonous vertical stack. The invisible grid narrows but stays asymmetric; edge-weighting and offset persist; the arrival image still breaks the fold; the capability counterpoint stacks without primacy; the focus traverses become touch-swipeable while preserving focus and the point-cloud inspection; the coordinate index and blue thread continue. Hierarchy, focus, rhythm, and the single primary action survive at every size. *Why:* "never let responsive views collapse into one monotonous stack that erases art-direction" (v2.0), while every task path stays obvious (usability).

## Part F — Accessibility & usability guardrails

Task-first Global Header always present and obvious. Focus interactions are fully keyboard-operable and announced; nothing essential is hidden behind hover alone; reduced-motion resolves all focus/reveal/point-cloud motion to instant static states with no loss of meaning or content. Contrast is ample (warm near-black on white; blue used at accessible contrast for its rare marks). The single primary action is unmistakable and reachable at any point via the header. The point cloud has a static, inspectable fallback. Discovery is *additive* — the whole page is fully understandable without performing a single interaction. *Why:* this is a commercial studio site; artistry never costs a visitor the ability to understand the practice or reach contact.

## Part G — Validation

Against the `VISUAL_DIRECTION_v2.0` acceptance checklist and the user's success criteria:

- **Unmistakably our brand** — pixel voice, electric blue, mascot, white ground, floating compositions, technical work: all present and matured ✔.
- **Unmistakably more mature** — one continuous survey with focus hierarchy and measured pacing, not stacked branded blocks ✔.
- **No resemblance to Septiembre** — the system is digital, interactive, blue-threaded, point-cloud-led; no print/monochrome/editorial-spread borrowing ✔.
- **No SaaS/agency/generic-architecture feeling** — no filled CTA blocks, no button footer, no KPI band, no card grid, no hero-with-scrim ✔.
- **No "sections stacked vertically"** — overlap/bleed, one grid, blue thread, coordinate index make it one composition ✔.
- **Stronger hierarchy / rhythm / discovery / focus** — focus is the engine; DENSE↔SILENT in space and time; the explorable point cloud; one-thing-in-focus throughout ✔.
- **Measured reality, warm not cold** — the survey metaphor, measured-data type, the human lines and the mascot keeping it warm; the technology reveals the architecture ✔.
- **Frozen architecture intact** — M-1…M-7 all present, responsibilities unchanged, module CTAs → canonical destinations, single primary action last, co-equal pillars, no invented components (the coordinate index and blue thread are visual treatments of existing wayfinding/measurement, not new architecture) ✔.
- **Usability preserved** — task-first header, obvious primary action, keyboard/reduced-motion support, additive discovery ✔.

## Self-review

**1. What changed compared to the previous Homepage HiFi.** Everything at the level of *feeling*. The previous version was a warm-beige, serif-display-led, centred page: a full-screen hero with a darkening scrim, evenly-stacked sections, a three-equal-card project grid, a KPI statistics band, a black filled CTA button, and a four-column footer. This version is white-ground and pixel/measured-data-led; it replaces the hero with a spacious *arrival* whose image breaks the fold; it replaces stacked sections with one continuous, overlapping survey bound by a blue measurement thread and a coordinate index; it replaces the card grid with focus traverses where one project is measured at a time; it replaces the KPI band with credibility-as-measured-facts; it replaces the black button with a single blue *activation*; and it replaces the four-column footer with a composed, mascot-led close.

**2. Which decisions came directly from the new philosophy.** Blue restricted to activation/measurement and used as the connective *thread* — directly from §2.1. Focus as the hierarchy engine across capabilities, work, and curated views — directly from §2.2. Motion as a deliberate instrument-grade material (slow focus, slow point cloud) — from §2.3. Silence as space *and* time, and reveals-as-inspection — from §2.4. Metadata as measured data and the pixel-as-coordinate index — from §2.5. Discovery as a co-equal value producing the explorable point cloud and inspection reveals — from §0.2. The whole "survey" narrative and warmth (human lines, the mascot) — from §0.1 and its warmth caveat.

**3. Which parts of the previous design I intentionally rejected.** The full-screen hero-with-scrim; centred, evenly-spaced sections; the three-equal-card grid; the KPI/statistics band; the black filled CTA button; the four-column utility footer; the warm-beige palette and serif-display-as-sophistication; and — most importantly — the *stacked-sections mentality* itself. Each was replaced because it either violated a v2.0 principle or read as template rather than instrument.

**4. Completely new ideas that emerged only from "measured reality."** The homepage-as-a-single-survey narrative; the **blue measurement thread** as continuity; the **coordinate index** as wayfinding-and-binding; **credibility rendered as field-noted measured data**; the **focus traverse** (measure one project at a time) as the work's structure; and the **explorable point cloud as the discovery centrepiece**, where the electric blue *is* the scan-line — the brand colour being literally what it depicts. None of these exist without the metaphor; all of them keep the technology in service of revealing the architecture.
